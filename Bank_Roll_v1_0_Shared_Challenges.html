Official Rules

Bank Roll: Break & Bank

  1. The app creates one challenge for the entire round.
  2. Every player gets one attempt at the same called ball, pocket, difficulty, and rail requirement.
  3. Rack three balls with the called ball at the front and any two object balls behind it.
  4. Break the rack, then bank the called ball into the required pocket.
  5. The called ball must meet the displayed rail requirement before entering the pocket.
  6. Wrong ball, wrong pocket, scratch, illegal contact, or failure to meet the challenge earns zero points.
  7. Golden Ball must be activated before the attempt. A made challenge doubles the player's points; a miss uses it and earns zero.
  8. Jackpot rounds double the base points for every player. Golden Ball can stack with Jackpot.
  9. When a player reaches the winning score, the round continues until everyone has had the same number of attempts.
