How to Play

Official Break & Bank rules.

  1. The app calls one object ball, one pocket, and a point value.
  2. Rack the called ball at the front of a three-ball mini-rack.
  3. Use any two other object balls behind it.
  4. Break the rack and let all balls stop.
  5. Bank the called ball into the designated pocket.
  6. The called ball must touch at least one rail before entering the pocket.
  7. A scratch, wrong pocket, wrong ball, or non-bank counts as a miss.
  8. Golden Ball must be activated before the break. A successful challenge earns double points. A miss uses the Golden Ball.
