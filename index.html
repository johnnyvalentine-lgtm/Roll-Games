Official Rules

Bank Roll: Break & Bank

  1. One shared challenge is generated for each round.
  2. Every player gets exactly one attempt at the same shared challenge. Everyone shoots the identical 9-ball rack, pocket, difficulty, rails, and special rule before a new challenge is generated.
  3. The 9 ball is always placed at the front of the three-ball rack. The app randomly selects two different balls to place behind it.
  4. Break the rack, then bank the called ball into the required pocket.
  5. Wrong ball, wrong pocket, scratch, illegal contact, or failure to satisfy the rule scores zero.
  6. Golden Ball doubles the player's points if made. A miss uses the Golden Ball and scores zero.
  7. Jackpot rounds double the base points for all players. Golden Ball stacks with Jackpot.
  8. After every player has taken one shot at the current challenge, a brand-new shared challenge is generated.
  9. When a player reaches the winning score, all remaining players complete the same final round.
